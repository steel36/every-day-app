Reflect
Elijah expected to find God in the wind, the earthquake, the fire — and instead found him in a whisper. Jesus describes a similar kind of listening: sheep who know their shepherd's voice well enough to recognize it anywhere, apart from every other voice. Hearing God rarely starts with something dramatic. It starts with staying quiet and familiar enough with his voice to notice it.
